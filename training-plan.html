PT EXERCISE REFERENCE

Speed Warmup — Before Every Single Run

  • Jog easy 2–3 min
  • High knees
  • Butt kicks
  • Side shuffle
  • Carioca
  • Forward hops + jog
  • Side-to-side hops
  • Rotational hops

Daily Stretching — Every Day, Run or Not

  • Calf stretch — both legs
  • Hamstring stretch elevated — forward, R, L per leg
  • Foam roll quads — 3 positions per leg
  • Figure four glute roll
  • Lacrosse ball — IT band, glute med, quads (after runs or before bed)

Hip Circuit — Strength Days Only

  • Butt blasters
  • Monster walks — forward & backward
  • Sumo walks — band on ankles
  • Wall ball glute med press — knee into ball, toe on ground
  • Single-leg RDL — roller between wall and opposite inside knee
  • Band lunge — blue band at hip, heel contact with ground
  • Band ankle lunge — band low at ankle, fwd & backward

Stability Progression — Part of Hip Circuit

  • Start on foam block (with shoes)
  • Progress to bosu (without shoes) as stability improves
  • Teeter totters — fwd/back, side-to-side, rotational
  • 3-type lunges with front foot on bosu
  • Single-leg squat on stepper — 5 directions per leg
